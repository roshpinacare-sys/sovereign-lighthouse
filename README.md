**ROLE (2026-10-11, task 10-c):** the **public heart-rail beacon** — the
GitHub-Pages mirror of the sovereign memory (the second cloud, twin of
`sovereign-beacon` on the GitLab rail; "two independent hearts" by design —
NOT a dead duplicate). Two heartbeats keep it alive: (1) the external heart
writer `sanbox/scripts/lighthouse-sync.mjs` (spawned by heart-tick every
~15min — secret-scans the payload, change-gates on the heart-chain sha,
verifies the Pages URL), and (2) since 2026-10-11 the self-contained keyless
`lighthouse-pulse.yml` workflow (T11) so the beacon is never stale even
without the sandbox. Why it was muted: it is a mirror, not an engine — zero
workflows of its own was by design; the wipeable-sandbox single-point risk
(writer lives outside) is recorded in the 5-d audit and is now mitigated by
the pulse. Revival/robustness path: re-home the writer when the operator's
machine returns; this Pages tree needs nothing.

זיכרון חוץ-מכונה עצמאי של מערכת ראש-פינה (T-59). נולד מריפו `sanbox` הפרטי; עץ סטטי זה מוכתב כל עדכון-משמעותי.
